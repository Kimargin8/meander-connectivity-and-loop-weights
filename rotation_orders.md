# Rotation orders in the two extremal Smith layers

**Corollary to the gap theorem.** Work on all labelled sites, with the full rotation operator \(R\) of order \(N=2k+1\), \(k\ge3\). In the graded multiplicity space of the Smith factor \(2^v\), group rotation eigenvalues by their exact order \(r\mid N\), after extending the residue field to \(\overline{\mathbb F}_2\). The dimensions below sum all eigenvalues of that order. Write \(\epsilon=[3\mid N]\), \(u=(\binom N3-\epsilon N/3)/N\), \(\delta=[r=1]\), and let \(\varphi\) be Euler's totient function. Use \(e=v_2((k+1)!)\) and \(a=v_2(k(k+1))\).

| parity | dimension at \(2^e\), exact order \(r\) | dimension at \(2^{e-a}\), exact order \(r\) |
|---|---|---|
| \(k\) odd | \(\delta\) | \((k+1)\varphi(r)-2\delta\) |
| \(k\) even | \(\varphi(r)+\delta\) | \((u+k-2+[3\mid N,\ r\mid N/3])\varphi(r)-2\delta\) |

**Proof.** Put \(C=\operatorname{coker}A\) for the labelled connected-gluing matrix. For \(v\ge1\) the quotient
\[
V_v=(2^{v-1}C)[2]\big/(2^v C)[2]
\]
has one binary dimension per Smith factor of exact order \(2^v\). Both exponents in the table are positive. For \(d\mid N\) the subgroup generated by \(R^d\) has odd order. Averaging over it is defined on 2-primary groups and over \(\mathbb Z_2\), splits invariants and commutes with all the operations in this quotient. Its invariant dimension on \(V_v\) is therefore the multiplicity of \(2^v\) in the orbit matrix of the subgroup. Since \(R\) has odd order, it is diagonalizable over the algebraic closure in characteristic two. The invariant dimension is precisely the sum of the dimensions for \(r\mid d\). This determines the table by inversion on divisors.

Concretely, a regular rotation orbit on \(N\) objects contributes \(d=\sum_{r\mid d}\varphi(r)\) subgroup orbits. The triples have \(u\) regular orbits. When \(3\mid N\) there is one further orbit, formed by translates of the unique subgroup of size three. It has size \(N/3\) and contributes \(\gcd(d,N/3)=\sum_{r\mid d,\ r\mid N/3}\varphi(r)\). Substitution in Theorem 1 gives the displayed formulas.

**Example.** At \(N=9\), the rotation orders \(1,3,9\) contribute \(2,2,6\) dimensions in the top layer and \(10,24,66\) dimensions in the next distinct layer. Their sums \(10,100\) are the two multiplicities for all labelled diagrams. In the order-three sector the extra triple orbit still contributes, whereas in the order-nine sector only the full-length triple orbits contribute.

The checker projects the geometric matrices for \(N=7,9\) directly onto each exact-order summand over a finite 2-adic quotient and computes their Smith histograms. The corollary determines the aggregate dimension at each order. Its proof uses no decomposition among different irreducible factors of a fixed cyclotomic polynomial over \(\mathbb F_2\).
