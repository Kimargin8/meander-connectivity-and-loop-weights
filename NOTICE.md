The connectivity paper specializes the periodic Gram determinant and spin map of Alexi Morin-Duchesne and Yvan Saint-Aubin and uses Thomas Bier's integral basis for subset incidence. The direct checks use integer diagram counts computed for this collection. [Periodic link modules](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/46/28/285207) · [Subset bases](https://doi.org/10.1006/eujc.1993.1001)

The loop-weight paper uses the irreducible meandric-system decomposition studied by Alexandru Nica and the finite-defect results of Motohisa Fukuda and Ion Nechita. The large-weight exponent was predicted by Philippe Di Francesco, Emmanuel Guitter and Jesper Lykke Jacobsen. The certificate generates its loop counts from combinatorial recurrences. [Irreducible systems](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0219025716500119) · [Large loop counts](https://doi.org/10.4171/AIHPD/80) · [Predicted asymptotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0003008)
